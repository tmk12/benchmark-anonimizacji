4. QUORUM. The chair declared that stockholders holding all 614 outstanding shares of common stock were present in person or by proxy, constituting a quorum.
5. The share capital of the Company is $30,700.00, divided into 614 shares of equal nominal value.
6. ACCOUNTS. The meeting received the annual accounts for the previous financial year and the report of William Lopez, the Company's auditor.
Mr. Lopez confirmed that the financial statements fairly present the Corporation's position.
7. PERFORMANCE. Carol Wilson presented the report on trading and the outlook for the coming year.
8. ELECTION OF DIRECTOR. Upon motion duly made and seconded, it was RESOLVED that Christina Allen be elected a director of the Corporation to serve until the next annual meeting. Votes for: 479; against: 135.
9. AMENDMENT OF BYLAWS. It was RESOLVED that the bylaws of the Corporation be amended as set out in the document presented at the meeting.
10. DIVIDEND. It was resolved that a dividend of $11,230.01 be paid to holders of shares pro rata to their holdings, in the next quarter.
11. There being no further business, the chair declared the meeting closed.
The next ordinary meeting was scheduled for 12/28/2021.
