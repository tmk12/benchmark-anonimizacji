MINUTES OF A GENERAL MEETING OF THE MEMBERS OF Silverstone Legal Ltd
Held at 76 Regent Avenue, Lichfield WS2 4GW on 19 January 2023
1. CALL TO ORDER. Stephen Hill took the chair and called the meeting to order. Robert King acted as secretary and recorded the minutes.
2. NOTICE. The chair noted that notice of the meeting had been sent to all members entitled to attend on the required notice period, in accordance with section 307 of the Companies Act 2006, and the notice was taken as read.
3. APOLOGIES. Gregory Burton had notified the secretary in advance that he could not attend.

4. QUORUM. The chair declared that a quorum was present, holders of all 1377 issued shares being represented in person or by proxy, so that the meeting was validly constituted.
5. The share capital of the Company is £68,850.00, divided into 1377 shares of equal nominal value.
6. ACCOUNTS. The meeting received the annual accounts for the previous financial year and the report of Justin Saunders, the Company's auditor.
Mr Saunders confirmed that the accounts give a true and fair view.
8. ORDINARY RESOLUTION. It was resolved by ordinary resolution that Steven Sanchez, of 12 Rectory Terrace, London SW3 5QP, be appointed a director of the Company with effect from the date of the meeting. Votes for: 826; against: 551.
9. SPECIAL RESOLUTION. It was resolved by special resolution, in accordance with section 283 of the Companies Act 2006, that the articles of association be amended as set out in the document tabled at the meeting.
10. DIVIDEND. It was resolved that a dividend of £13,483.21 be paid to holders of shares pro rata to their holdings, in the next quarter.
11. There being no further business, the chair declared the meeting closed.
These minutes, once signed by the chair, are evidence of the proceedings under section 356 of the Companies Act 2006 and will be kept at the registered office for at least ten years.
