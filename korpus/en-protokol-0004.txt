4. QUORUM. The chair declared that a quorum was present, holders of all 1016 issued shares being represented in person or by proxy, so that the meeting was validly constituted.
5. The share capital of the Company is £50,800.00, divided into 1016 shares of equal nominal value.
6. ACCOUNTS. The meeting received the annual accounts for the previous financial year and the report of Connor Hudson, the Company's auditor.
Mr Hudson confirmed that the accounts give a true and fair view.
7. PERFORMANCE. Susan Holland presented the report on trading and the outlook for the coming year.

10. DIVIDEND. It was resolved that a dividend of £13,950.01 be paid to holders of shares pro rata to their holdings, in the next quarter.

The next ordinary meeting was scheduled for 06/12/2021.
