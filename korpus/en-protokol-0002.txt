MINUTES OF A SPECIAL MEETING OF THE STOCKHOLDERS OF Quayside Inc.
Held at the offices of the Company, 4936 Washington Lane, Lexington, KY 40596, on November 2, 2021
1. CALL TO ORDER. Mary Adams took the chair and called the meeting to order. Brian Mendoza acted as secretary and recorded the minutes.
2. NOTICE. The secretary confirmed that written notice of the time, place and purpose of the meeting had been mailed to every stockholder of record not less than ten nor more than sixty days before the meeting, as required by Section 222 of the Delaware General Corporation Law.
3. APOLOGIES. Hannah Byrd had notified the secretary in advance that she could not attend.

4. QUORUM. The chair declared that stockholders holding all 969 outstanding shares of common stock were present in person or by proxy, constituting a quorum.
5. The share capital of the Company is $48,450.00, divided into 969 shares of equal nominal value.
6. ACCOUNTS. The meeting received the annual accounts for the previous financial year and the report of Lauren Lewis, the Company's auditor.
Ms. Lewis confirmed that the financial statements fairly present the Corporation's position.
7. PERFORMANCE. Richard Wilson presented the report on trading and the outlook for the coming year.
8. ELECTION OF DIRECTOR. Upon motion duly made and seconded, it was RESOLVED that David Elliott be elected a director of the Corporation to serve until the next annual meeting. Votes for: 745; against: 224.
9. AMENDMENT OF BYLAWS. It was RESOLVED that the bylaws of the Corporation be amended as set out in the document presented at the meeting.
10. DIVIDEND. It was resolved that a dividend of $15,976.40 be paid to holders of shares pro rata to their holdings, in the next quarter.
11. There being no further business, the chair declared the meeting closed.
The next ordinary meeting was scheduled for August 9, 2022.
