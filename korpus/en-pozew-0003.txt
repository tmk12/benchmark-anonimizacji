COMPLAINT FOR BREACH OF CONTRACT AND DEMAND FOR JURY TRIAL
1. This Court has subject-matter jurisdiction over this action and personal jurisdiction over Defendant. Venue is proper in this county under the applicable state venue statute because the loan was negotiated and was to be repaid here.
2. Plaintiff Melissa Morris is an individual residing at 1409 Wordsworth Road, Boise, ID 83781. Defendant William Tucker is an individual residing at 5560 Dock Way, Boise, ID 83703.
3. On 03/02/2018, Plaintiff and Defendant entered into a written loan agreement under which Plaintiff lent Defendant $28,871.90, to be repaid by the date stated in the agreement. Plaintiff wired the funds to Defendant's account (Routing No. 298714881, Account No. 9462033564) the same day.
4. The loan fell due for repayment on 08/14/2018. The Defendant did not repay it.
5. On 09/21/2018, Plaintiff's counsel sent Defendant a written demand by certified mail to 5560 Dock Way, Boise, ID 83703, giving fourteen days to pay. Defendant did not respond.
6. In or around the same period the Defendant acknowledged the debt in conversation with Barbara Hernandez and later in a telephone call witnessed by Deborah Castro (tel. +1 208-555-0191).
7. A prior action concerning the same loan was filed as Case No. CGC-18-534608 and was voluntarily dismissed without prejudice.
8. Defendant has breached the loan agreement by failing to repay the principal when due. Plaintiff has performed all of its obligations under the agreement and has been damaged in the amount of the unpaid principal together with pre-judgment interest as permitted by law.
WHEREFORE, Plaintiff respectfully requests that the Court enter judgment in its favor and against Defendant as follows:
(a) damages in the amount of $28,871.90;
(b) pre-judgment and post-judgment interest at the maximum rate permitted by law, running from 08/14/2018;
(c) the costs of this action, including reasonable attorneys' fees where permitted by contract or statute; and such other relief as the Court deems just and proper.
A filing fee of $1,443.60 has been paid to the Clerk of Court.
