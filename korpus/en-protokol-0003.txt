MINUTES OF A SPECIAL MEETING OF THE STOCKHOLDERS OF Evergreen LLC
Held at the offices of the Company, 8955 Tudor Lane, Wichita, KS 67219, on August 12, 2021
1. CALL TO ORDER. John Walsh took the chair and called the meeting to order. Stephanie Castro acted as secretary and recorded the minutes.
2. NOTICE. The secretary confirmed that written notice of the time, place and purpose of the meeting had been mailed to every stockholder of record not less than ten nor more than sixty days before the meeting, as required by Section 222 of the Delaware General Corporation Law.
3. APOLOGIES. Jacob Barker had notified the secretary in advance that he could not attend.

4. QUORUM. The chair declared that stockholders holding all 1539 outstanding shares of common stock were present in person or by proxy, constituting a quorum.
5. The share capital of the Company is $76,950.00, divided into 1539 shares of equal nominal value.
6. ACCOUNTS. The meeting received the annual accounts for the previous financial year and the report of Joseph Walker, the Company's auditor.
7. PERFORMANCE. Jerry Brown presented the report on trading and the outlook for the coming year.
8. ELECTION OF DIRECTOR. Upon motion duly made and seconded, it was RESOLVED that Harrison Brown be elected a director of the Corporation to serve until the next annual meeting. Votes for: 1143; against: 396.
9. AMENDMENT OF BYLAWS. It was RESOLVED that the bylaws of the Corporation be amended as set out in the document presented at the meeting.
10. DIVIDEND. It was resolved that a dividend of $23,410.81 be paid to holders of shares pro rata to their holdings, in the next quarter.
The next ordinary meeting was scheduled for August 2, 2022.
