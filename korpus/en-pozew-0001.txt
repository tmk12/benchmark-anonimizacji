PARTICULARS OF CLAIM
1. The Claimant's claim is for repayment of a loan and interest. The value of the claim exceeds £100,000 and the Claimant therefore issues in the High Court in accordance with the Practice Direction to CPR Part 7. The Defendant is domiciled in England and Wales.
2. The Claimant is an individual resident at 107 Wordsworth Way, Birmingham B3 3NH. The Defendant is an individual resident at 183 Cedar Street, Sheffield S1 8UX.
3. By a written loan agreement dated 31/01/2020 the Claimant lent the Defendant the sum of £299,805.36, repayable by the date stated in the agreement. The sum was paid to the Defendant's account GB29LOYD30029985682055 on the day the agreement was signed.
4. The loan fell due for repayment on 07/12/2020. The Defendant did not repay it.
5. On 14/12/2020 the Claimant's solicitors sent the Defendant a letter before claim to 183 Cedar Street, Sheffield S1 8UX, giving fourteen days to pay. The Defendant did not reply.
6. In or around the same period the Defendant acknowledged the debt in conversation with Larry Craig and later in a telephone call witnessed by Ryan Rodriguez (tel. +44 118 496 0123).
7. Separate interim injunction proceedings were previously brought in claim number Claim No. QB-2021-603222; those proceedings were concluded by consent.
8. The Defendant is in breach of the loan agreement. The Claimant is entitled to repayment of the principal and to interest under section 69 of the County Courts Act 1984 (alternatively section 35A of the Senior Courts Act 1981) at such rate and for such period as the Court thinks fit.
AND THE CLAIMANT CLAIMS:
(1) The sum of £299,805.36 by way of repayment of the loan;
(2) Interest pursuant to the Court's statutory power, at the rate of 8% a year from 07/12/2020 until judgment or earlier payment;
(3) Costs.
The court fee payable on issue is £14,990.27.
