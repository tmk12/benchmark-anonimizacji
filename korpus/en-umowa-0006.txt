1.1 The Firm shall provide the Client with general legal advice on commercial, employment and data protection matters, including drafting and reviewing contracts, preparing written advice and attending negotiations on the Client's behalf (the “Services”).

2. FEES AND PAYMENT
2.1 In consideration of the Services the Client shall pay the Firm a fixed monthly retainer of $11,951.00, payable in advance on the first business day of each month.
2.2 The fixed fee covers up to twenty hours of lawyers' time per month. Time beyond that allowance is charged at the Firm's standard hourly rates, and additional charges in any month shall not exceed $5,839.36 without the Client's prior written approval.
2.3 Invoices are payable within fourteen days of receipt by transfer to the Firm's account Routing No. 041437962, Account No. 579340288, quoting the invoice number as the payment reference.
2.4 Amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one percent per month and the maximum rate permitted by applicable law.

3.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other in connection with this Agreement and shall use it only to perform or receive the Services.
3.3 Nothing in this clause limits the Firm's professional duties of confidentiality and privilege owed to the Client, which continue after termination.
