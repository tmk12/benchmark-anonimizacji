4. QUORUM. The chair declared that a quorum was present, holders of all 988 issued shares being represented in person or by proxy, so that the meeting was validly constituted.
5. The share capital of the Company is £49,400.00, divided into 988 shares of equal nominal value.
6. ACCOUNTS. The meeting received the annual accounts for the previous financial year and the report of Lisa Green, the Company's auditor.
7. PERFORMANCE. Olivia George presented the report on trading and the outlook for the coming year.
8. ORDINARY RESOLUTION. It was resolved by ordinary resolution that Eric Pittman, of 211 Poplar Crescent, Wolverhampton WV2 3AY, be appointed a director of the Company with effect from the date of the meeting. Votes for: 621; against: 367.
9. SPECIAL RESOLUTION. It was resolved by special resolution, in accordance with section 283 of the Companies Act 2006, that the articles of association be amended as set out in the document tabled at the meeting.
10. DIVIDEND. It was resolved that a dividend of £15,745.70 be paid to holders of shares pro rata to their holdings, in the next quarter.
The next ordinary meeting was scheduled for 17/05/2027.
