PARTICULARS OF CLAIM
1. The Claimant's claim is for repayment of a loan and interest. The value of the claim is less than £100,000 and this Court has jurisdiction under section 15 of the County Courts Act 1984. The Claimant's address for service is 232 South Avenue, Derby DE2 1DX.
2. The Claimant is an individual resident at 232 South Avenue, Derby DE2 1DX. The Defendant is an individual resident at 67 Cromwell Road, Crawley RH4 6LR.
3. By a written loan agreement dated 24 September 2021 the Claimant lent the Defendant the sum of £31,450.71, repayable by the date stated in the agreement. The sum was paid to the Defendant's account GB16SRLG60112675040380 on the day the agreement was signed.
4. The loan fell due for repayment on 13 October 2021. The Defendant did not repay it.
5. On 19 October 2021 the Claimant's solicitors sent the Defendant a letter before claim to 67 Cromwell Road, Crawley RH4 6LR, giving fourteen days to pay. The Defendant did not reply.
6. In or around the same period the Defendant acknowledged the debt in conversation with James King and later in a telephone call witnessed by Samuel Franklin (tel. +44 116 496 0234).

8. The Defendant is in breach of the loan agreement. The Claimant is entitled to repayment of the principal and to interest under section 69 of the County Courts Act 1984 (alternatively section 35A of the Senior Courts Act 1981) at such rate and for such period as the Court thinks fit.

(1) The sum of £31,450.71 by way of repayment of the loan;
