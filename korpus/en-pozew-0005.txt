COMPLAINT FOR BREACH OF CONTRACT AND DEMAND FOR JURY TRIAL
1. This Court has subject-matter jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1332(a): the amount in controversy exceeds $75,000, exclusive of interest and costs, and the parties are alleged to be citizens of different States. Venue is proper under 28 U.S.C. § 1391(b).
2. Plaintiff Mark Rodriguez is an individual residing at 6175 Holly Court, Boise, ID 83767. Defendant Matthew White is an individual residing at 134 Grange Street, Eugene, OR 97460.
3. On 03/10/2023, Plaintiff and Defendant entered into a written loan agreement under which Plaintiff lent Defendant $312,370.96, to be repaid by the date stated in the agreement. Plaintiff wired the funds to Defendant's account (Routing No. 245261950, Account No. 893480260) the same day.

7. A prior action concerning the same loan was filed as Case No. 8:23-cv-86828-UCL and was voluntarily dismissed without prejudice.
