MINUTES OF A GENERAL MEETING OF THE MEMBERS OF Cedar Point Ltd
Held at 155 Chancery Close, London SW4 9JH on 12 February 2024
1. CALL TO ORDER. Mark Howard took the chair and called the meeting to order. Kathleen Williams acted as secretary and recorded the minutes.
2. NOTICE. The chair noted that notice of the meeting had been sent to all members entitled to attend on the required notice period, in accordance with section 307 of the Companies Act 2006, and the notice was taken as read.
3. APOLOGIES. Harry Brooks had notified the secretary in advance that he could not attend.

4. QUORUM. The chair declared that a quorum was present, holders of all 991 issued shares being represented in person or by proxy, so that the meeting was validly constituted.
5. The share capital of the Company is £49,550.00, divided into 991 shares of equal nominal value.
6. ACCOUNTS. The meeting received the annual accounts for the previous financial year and the report of Terry Robinson, the Company's auditor.
Mr Robinson confirmed that the accounts give a true and fair view.
8. ORDINARY RESOLUTION. It was resolved by ordinary resolution that Jennifer Guerrero, of 31 London Grove, Southampton SO1 7UZ, be appointed a director of the Company with effect from the date of the meeting. Votes for: 918; against: 73.
9. SPECIAL RESOLUTION. It was resolved by special resolution, in accordance with section 283 of the Companies Act 2006, that the articles of association be amended as set out in the document tabled at the meeting.
10. DIVIDEND. It was resolved that a dividend of £13,756.65 be paid to holders of shares pro rata to their holdings, in the next quarter.
11. There being no further business, the chair declared the meeting closed.
The next ordinary meeting was scheduled for 14 August 2024.
